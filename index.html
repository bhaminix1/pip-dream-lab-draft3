HOW PIP GOT THE JOB

scroll slowly. the story wakes up as you go.

  1. 06:00 · CHAPTER 1

    The Sleepy Sunrise

    Pip wakes up just as the sun is yawning. Everything is quiet and golden. Even the clocks are whispering, and one of them is snoring.

  2. 06:07 · CHAPTER 2

    A Tiny Glow

    Inside a lonely sock, Pip finds a star no bigger than a button. It hiccups sparkles. Pip decides, very firmly, to keep it.

  3. 06:21 · CHAPTER 3

    Collecting Things

    Pip starts gathering dreams the way other babies gather crumbs: carefully, and mostly by accident. Starlight goes in the left pocket. Hiccups go in the right.

  4. 06:48 · CHAPTER 4

    The First Tiny Dream

    One star, two hums and a spoonful of sunshine. Pip stirs gently. Out comes a dream about a very polite marshmallow. It works!

  5. 07:30 · CHAPTER 5

    Building the Lab

    Pip borrows a blanket fort, a retro computer found in a cloud, and nine sunbeams in jars. The Sunshine Dream Lab opens. It is cozy and slightly crooked.

  6. 08:15 · CHAPTER 6

    Official Business

    Pip files a very serious form (in crayon). Title granted: Junior Dream Engineer. The Senior role is pending, and requires approximately forty more naps.

  7. 09:00 · CHAPTER 7

    Come In, Quietly

    Pip props the door open with a pacifier. "shhh... come in. mind the stars on the floor, and please say hi to the sun on your way past."
